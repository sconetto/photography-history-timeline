Alfred Harman began making [[gelatin-dry-plate|gelatin dry plates]] in the town of Ilford, east of London, in 1879. The firm took the town's name and became Britain's main maker of film and photographic paper.

Its black-and-white films, such as FP4 and HP5, and its papers were used by generations of darkroom workers. After it collapsed in 2004, managers bought the black-and-white business and formed [[m:harman|Harman Technology]].

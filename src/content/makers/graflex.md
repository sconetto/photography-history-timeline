William Folmer and William Schwing started in New York making gas lamps and bicycles, then cameras. Their Graflex reflex cameras and the folding [[speed-graphic|Speed Graphic]] became the standard tools of American press photographers. The company was part of Kodak from 1905 to 1926 and later moved to Rochester.

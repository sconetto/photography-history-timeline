Voigtländer was founded in Vienna in 1756 by Johann Christoph Voigtländer, making optical instruments. In 1840–41 it built and sold the [[petzval-lens|Petzval portrait lens]] with an all-metal daguerreotype camera, the first commercial camera designed for a calculated lens.

The firm moved to Braunschweig, Germany, in 1849. The Carl Zeiss Foundation took control in 1956, and camera making ended in 1972.

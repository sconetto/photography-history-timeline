Edwin Land founded Polaroid in 1937 to make polarising filters. In 1947 he showed a camera that developed its own picture in about a minute, and the Model 95 went on sale in 1948.

The 1972 SX-70 made pictures that developed in your hand. Digital cameras took away Polaroid's market, and after bankruptcies in 2001 and 2008 it stopped making instant film. The [[m:impossible|Impossible Project]] bought its last film factory.

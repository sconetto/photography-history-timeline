Ihagee, from the German letters of Industrie- und Handels-Gesellschaft, was founded in Dresden in 1912 by the Dutchman Johan Steenbergen. It made the Exakta and the [[kine-exakta|Kine Exakta]], among the first 35mm single-lens reflexes.

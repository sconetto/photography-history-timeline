## Beginnings

OM Digital Solutions grew out of the [[m:olympus|Olympus]] camera business. Camera sales had been falling for years as phones took over everyday photography. In 2020 it agreed to sell the business to Japan Industrial Partners, a Japanese investment firm, and set up a new company to hold it. OM Digital Solutions took over the cameras, lenses and audio recorders on 1 January 2021.

The new company inherited more than eight decades of Olympus camera making: the Zuiko lenses, the Pen and OM names, the Tough waterproof compacts, and the [[four-thirds|Micro Four Thirds]] system Olympus had launched with [[m:panasonic|Panasonic]] in 2008. Its first new cameras, such as the Pen E-P7 of 2021, still carried the Olympus name.

## Growth and turning points

In 2021 the company announced its own brand, OM System. Its name echoes the OM film SLRs of the 1970s. The first camera to carry it, the OM System OM-1 of 2022, was a new flagship: a stacked [[g:mirrorless|mirrorless]] sensor fast enough for 50 frames a second, phase-detection autofocus across the frame, and a body sealed against dust, rain and frost.

The company leaned into what a small sensor and a small company could do well. Its lenses are light for their reach: the M.Zuiko 150–400mm of 2021 gives the view of a 1000 mm lens on full frame and can still be held by hand. Its cameras do in the body what used to need a computer afterwards. "Live ND" imitates a neutral-density filter by combining frames, "Live GND" does the same for a graduated filter, and other modes stack frames for higher resolution, deeper focus or brighter star trails.

## What it made

OM Digital Solutions makes Micro Four Thirds cameras and M.Zuiko lenses, rugged Tough compacts and audio recorders. Its main customers are nature, wildlife and outdoor photographers who value small, weatherproof equipment.

It has also built cameras for special jobs: the E-M1 Mark III Astro of 2023 has a filter tuned to record the red light of hydrogen nebulae, and business versions of its cameras serve sterile medical settings, digital archiving and infrared inspection.

## Today

The OM-3 of 2025 borrowed the look of the 1972 OM-1, with dials on top and the pointed viewfinder hump, and the OM-5 Mark II followed. In 2025 the company reorganised into three businesses: OM System for cameras, OM Optical Precision for lens and optics manufacturing, and OM Inno-Techs for new businesses built on its technology. With Panasonic, it is one of the two main makers still building Micro Four Thirds cameras.

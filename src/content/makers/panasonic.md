Konosuke Matsushita founded the company in Osaka in 1918 to make electrical sockets. It made video cameras for decades, and in 2001 launched the Lumix digital cameras, with Leica-branded lenses. Its [[lumix-g1|Lumix G1]] of 2008 was the first mirrorless interchangeable-lens camera.

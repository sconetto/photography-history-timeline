Antoine Lumière and his sons Auguste and Louis built a photographic plate factory in Lyon in 1882. Its Étiquette bleue dry plates made the family rich. The brothers then invented the Cinématographe in 1895 and the [[autochrome|Autochrome]] colour plate, sold from 1907.

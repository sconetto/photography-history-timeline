Edward Weston, an English-born electrical engineer (not the photographer), founded the Weston Electrical Instrument Company in Newark, New Jersey, in 1888. In 1932 it launched the Weston 617, one of the first photoelectric [[light-meter|exposure meters]], and its film speed ratings were widely used before ISO.

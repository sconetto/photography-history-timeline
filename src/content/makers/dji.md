DJI was founded in Shenzhen in 2006 by Frank Wang. Its camera drones made aerial photography, once a matter of balloons, kites or chartered aircraft, something anyone could do. It also makes stabilised handheld cameras and owns most of [[m:hasselblad|Hasselblad]].

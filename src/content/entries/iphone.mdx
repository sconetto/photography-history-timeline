## How it began

[[camera-phone|Camera phones]] had existed since 1999–2000, with small screens and awkward menus. Apple’s iPhone of 2007 had a weak camera but a large touchscreen and a real web browser.

## How it works

A tiny [[cmos-sensor|CMOS sensor]] and fixed lens sat in a slim phone. What changed photography was software: the screen for viewing and editing, the App Store from 2008, and fast connections for sharing straight away.

## Why it mattered

The camera people always carried became good enough, and pictures could be shared instantly. Each model improved the camera, and apps such as Instagram grew around it. Sales of compact cameras collapsed from about 2010.

## Limits and what replaced it

Small sensors gather little light, so phones turned to [[computational-photography|computational photography]], combining many frames to make up for it.

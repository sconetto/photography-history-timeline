## How it began

In 2010 Fujifilm, which still made film, showed a digital compact that looked like a classic rangefinder such as the [[leica-m3|Leica M3]]. It went on sale in 2011.

## How it works

A fixed 23 mm lens on an [[aps-c|APS-C]] sensor gives the view of a 35 mm lens on full frame. Its viewfinder can switch between an optical view with frame lines and an electronic view of the sensor. Shutter speed and exposure are set with dials, not menus.

## Why it mattered

It showed there was demand for cameras designed around the experience of photographing. Fujifilm built its X system on it, and its film-simulation colours, based on its own films, became a selling point.

## Limits and what replaced it

The first model was slow to focus and had quirks. Later versions fixed most of them, and the line continues.

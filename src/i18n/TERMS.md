# Portuguese (pt-BR) terminology

The words chosen for the Brazilian Portuguese translation, so 45,000 words stay consistent.
When a term changes here, change it everywhere in `src/content/i18n/pt-br/` and `src/i18n/ui/pt-br.ts`.

## Voice

- Natural Brazilian Portuguese with the same plain, short sentences as the English. Address the reader as **você**.
- Dates: "c. 1826" stays "c. 1826"; BCE is **a.C.** ("400 a.C."). Decades: "anos 1990".
- Numbers use the decimal comma (1,5×; f/2,8). Thousands use a dot only from 10.000.
- Quotes stay in the original language, followed by a translation in brackets or in the sentence.
- Book, paper and artwork titles stay in the original, in italics. Add a short gloss in brackets on first mention where it helps.
- Proper names, companies and products are never translated (Kodak Brownie, Nikon F, Leica I).
